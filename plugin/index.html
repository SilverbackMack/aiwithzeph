The Zeph Song Maker turns Claude into a song kit. It gives you ideas, writes the lyrics and a music prompt, draws the cover and builds a lyric video page.

Download the plugin (.md) Skill zip
  1. Open a new chat at claude.ai and attach zeph-song-maker.md (or paste its text).
  2. Type start, pick an idea, then pick the length and speed.
  3. Reply approved to move to each next step.
  4. Paste the style prompt and lyrics into a music tool (Suno, Gemini, Google Flow Music or ElevenLabs Music), then type done.

You can also upload the zip as a Skill in Claude's settings, under Capabilities, where your plan allows it.
