Clone your own voice with AI

Type anything and hear it read back in your own voice. It is ideal for voiceovers, videos and podcasts when you don't have time to record.

  1. Start a clone. Go to elevenlabs.io, open Voices, press + and choose Instant Voice Clone.
  2. Record 1 to 2 minutes. Record or upload clear audio of you speaking naturally, with no music and no background noise. Quality matters more than length.
  3. Name it and save. Confirm it is your voice and press Save voice. It's ready in seconds. Then open Text to Speech, pick your voice, type a script and press Generate.

Honest note: the free plan gives you text to speech (10,000 credits a month), but voice cloning needs the Starter plan, about $6 a month (checked November 2026). Only ever clone your own voice, or one you have clear permission to use.
